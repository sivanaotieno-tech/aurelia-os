Internet Tools: Privacy Policy

It is important for us, Internet Tools developers, to let you know that we respect and care about your privacy and personal information. In this document, we describe how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our software and services.

1. Information to be collected and method of collection

We collect information required to ensure the app works properly. Device-specific information (such as your OS, screen size, memory size, device model, and manufacturer), IP address, cookies, date of visit, and service usage records may be generated automatically and collected during your use of the service.

2. Purposes of collecting and using personal information

We collect and use information to provide and improve our services, specifically:

  • To help facilitate user access to the service
  • To create statistical data regarding service usage
  • To analyze metrics for ongoing service improvements

3. Period of possession and use of personal information

Internet Tools shall, in principle, destroy the user’s personal information immediately once the purpose of collection and usage has been fulfilled.

To provide you with our services, we may store, process, and transmit information in locations worldwide—including locations outside your country. Information may also be stored locally on the devices you use to access the services.

4. Destruction of personal information

Internet Tools will immediately destroy personal information once its purpose has been achieved. Soft-copy output containing personal information will be shredded, and electronic files will be permanently deleted using technical measures that prevent record recovery.

5. Sharing and provision of personal information

Internet Tools will not provide user personal information to third parties, except as required in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Internet Tools may entrust third parties with handling personal information when necessary for the seamless provision of our services, including:

  • Facebook Audience Network
  • AppLovin Ad Network

6. Contacting us regarding this privacy policy

Please visit our website for more details on how to get in touch with our team.
